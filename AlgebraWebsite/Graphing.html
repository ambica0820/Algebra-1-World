Graphing

What is a Coordinate Plane?

A coordinate plane is a two-dimensional grid used to locate points using ordered pairs.

Writing Coordinates

A point is written as (x, y), where x represents the horizontal position and y represents the vertical position.

  • The x-coordinate tells the horizontal position on the X-axis.
  • The y-coordinate tells the vertical position on the Y-axis.

We always write the x coordinate first, followed by the y coordinate.

The Four Quadrants

The coordinate plane is divided into four sections called quadrants.

  • Quadrant I: x is positive and y is positive
  • Quadrant II: x is negative and y is positive
  • Quadrant III: x is negative and y is negative
  • Quadrant IV: x is positive and y is negative

Plotting Points

To plot a point on the coordinate plane, we follow the ordered pair (x, y).

  1. Start at the origin (0,0).
  2. Move left or right based on the x-coordinate.
  3. Move up or down based on the y-coordinate.
  4. Place a point at the final location.

Example 1:

Plot the point (3, 2).

  1. The x-coordinate is 3, so move 3 units to the right.
  2. The y-coordinate is 2, so move 2 units up.
  3. The point is located in Quadrant I.
