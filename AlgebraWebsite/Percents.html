What is a Percent?

A percent is a number that represents a part out of 100. The word percent means "per hundred" or "out of 100".

The symbol for percent is %.

Examples

  • 25% means 25 out of 100.
  • 50% means 50 out of 100.
  • 75% means 75 out of 100.
  • 100% means 100 out of 100, or the whole amount.

Percent as a Fraction

Since a percent represents a number out of 100, you can write a percent as a fraction with 100 as the denominator.

For example:

25% = 25/100

50% = 50/100

75% = 75/100

Think of It This Way

Imagine a pizza divided into 100 equal pieces. If you have 25 of those pieces, you have 25% of the pizza.

So, percentages are simply another way of describing parts of a whole.

Turning a Percent to a Decimal

To change a percent into a decimal, divide the percent by 100.

You can also move the decimal point two places to the left and remove the percent sign.

Examples

25% = 0.25

50% = 0.50

75% = 0.75

8% = 0.08

125% = 1.25

Step-by-Step

  1. Remove the percent sign (%).
  2. Move the decimal point two places to the left.
  3. Add a zero if necessary.

Example

Convert 35% to a decimal.

35% = 0.35

Therefore, 35% = 0.35.

Remember

Percent -> Decimal = Move the decimal 2 places left.

Turning a Decimal to a Percent

To change a decimal into a percent, multiply the decimal by 100.

You can also move the decimal point two places to the right and add the percent sign (%).

Examples

0.25 = 25%

0.50 = 50%

0.75 = 75%

0.08 = 8%

1.25 = 125%

Step-by-Step

  1. Move the decimal point two places to the right.
  2. Add the percent sign (%).

Example

Convert 0.35 to a percent.

0.35 -> 35%

Therefore, 0.35 = 35%.

Turning a Percent to a Fraction

To change a percent into a fraction, write the percent as a fraction with 100 as the denominator.

Then, simplify the fraction if possible.

Examples

25% = 25/100 = 1/4

50% = 50/100 = 1/2

75% = 75/100 = 3/4

20% = 20/100 = 1/5

10% = 10/100 = 1/10

Step-by-Step

  1. Remove the percent sign (%).
  2. Write the number over 100.
  3. Simplify the fraction.

Example

Convert 40% to a fraction.

40% = 40/100

Simplify by dividing both the numerator and denominator by 20:

40/100 = 2/5

Therefore, 40% = 2/5.

Turning a Fraction to a Percent

To change a fraction into a percent, first convert the fraction into a decimal. Then, multiply the decimal by 100.

You can also divide the numerator by the denominator and move the decimal point two places to the right.

Examples

1/2 = 0.50 = 50%

1/4 = 0.25 = 25%

3/4 = 0.75 = 75%

1/5 = 0.20 = 20%

3/5 = 0.60 = 60%

Step-by-Step

  1. Divide the numerator by the denominator.
  2. Convert the decimal to a percent by moving the decimal point two places to the right.
  3. Add the percent sign (%).

Example

Convert 2/5 to a percent.

2 ÷ 5 = 0.40

0.40 = 40%

Therefore, 2/5 = 40%.

Finding a Percent of a Number

To find a percent of a number, first convert the percent to a decimal. Then, multiply the decimal by the number.

Formula

Percent × Number = Answer

Examples

25% of 80

25% = 0.25

0.25 × 80 = 20

Therefore, 25% of 80 = 20.

40% of 50

40% = 0.40

0.40 × 50 = 20

Therefore, 40% of 50 = 20.

Step-by-Step

  1. Convert the percent to a decimal.
  2. Multiply the decimal by the given number.
  3. Write your answer.

Example

Find 15% of 200.

15% = 0.15

0.15 × 200 = 30

Therefore, 15% of 200 = 30.

Finding What Percent One Number Is of Another

To find what percent one number is of another, divide the first number by the second number. Then, multiply the decimal by 100.

Formula

(Part ÷ Whole) × 100 = Percent

Examples

What percent is 20 of 80?

20 ÷ 80 = 0.25

0.25 × 100 = 25%

Therefore, 20 is 25% of 80.

What percent is 15 of 60?

15 ÷ 60 = 0.25

0.25 × 100 = 25%

Therefore, 15 is 25% of 60.

Step-by-Step

  1. Identify the part and the whole.
  2. Divide the part by the whole.
  3. Multiply the decimal by 100.
  4. Add the percent sign (%).

Example

What percent is 30 of 120?

30 ÷ 120 = 0.25

0.25 × 100 = 25%

Therefore, 30 is 25% of 120.

Finding the Whole

When you know the percent and the part, you can find the whole by dividing the part by the percent written as a decimal.

Formula

Whole = Part ÷ Percent

Examples

25% of what number is 20?

25% = 0.25

20 ÷ 0.25 = 80

Therefore, 25% of 80 is 20.

40% of what number is 32?

40% = 0.40

32 ÷ 0.40 = 80

Therefore, 40% of 80 is 32.

Step-by-Step

  1. Convert the percent to a decimal.
  2. Divide the given part by the decimal.
  3. Write your answer as the whole.

Example

15% of what number is 30?

15% = 0.15

30 ÷ 0.15 = 200

Therefore, 15% of 200 is 30.

Percent Increase and Decrease

Percent increase tells us how much a value has INCREASED compared to its original value.

Percent decrease tells us how much a value has DECREASED compared to its original value.

FORMULAS

1. Percent Change = (Change in Value ÷ Original Value) × 100

2. Change in Value = New Value − Original Value

Percent Increase

To find the percent increase, subtract the original value from the new value. Then divide the change by the original value and multiply by 100.

Example 1

A shirt originally costs $40. The price increases to $50. What is the percent increase?

50 − 40 = 10

(10 ÷ 40) × 100 = 25%

Therefore, the price increased by 25%.

Example 2

A student's test score increases from 60 to 75. What is the percent increase?

75 − 60 = 15

(15 ÷ 60) × 100 = 25%

Therefore, the test score increased by 25%.

Example 3

The number of students in a club increases from 80 to 100. What is the percent increase?

100 − 80 = 20

(20 ÷ 80) × 100 = 25%

Therefore, the number of students increased by 25%.

Percent Decrease

To find the percent decrease, subtract the new value from the original value. Then divide the change by the original value and multiply by 100.

Example 1

A jacket originally costs $80. The price decreases to $60. What is the percent decrease?

80 − 60 = 20

(20 ÷ 80) × 100 = 25%

Therefore, the price decreased by 25%.

Example 2

A town's population decreases from 2,000 to 1,600. What is the percent decrease?

2,000 − 1,600 = 400

(400 ÷ 2,000) × 100 = 20%

Therefore, the population decreased by 20%.

Example 3

A phone's battery level decreases from 90% to 54%. What is the percent decrease?

90 − 54 = 36

(36 ÷ 90) × 100 = 40%

Therefore, the battery level decreased by 40%.

Step-by-Step

  1. Find the change in value.
  2. Divide the change by the original value.
  3. Multiply by 100.
  4. Add the percent sign (%).

Discounts and Sales Tax

Discounts and sales tax are common real-world applications of percentages. A discount DECREASES the original price, while sales tax INCREASES the original price.

Discounts

A discount is an amount taken off the original price of an item. Discounts are usually given as a percentage of the original price.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Rate

Sale Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Discount Example 1

A shirt costs $40 and is on sale for 25% off. What is the discount amount and the sale price?

25% = 0.25

$40 × 0.25 = $10

$40 − $10 = $30

Therefore, the discount is $10 and the sale price is $30.

Discount Example 2

A pair of shoes costs $80 and is on sale for 30% off. What is the discount amount and the sale price?

30% = 0.30

$80 × 0.30 = $24

$80 − $24 = $56

Therefore, the discount is $24 and the sale price is $56.

Discount Example 3

A jacket costs $120 and is on sale for 40% off. What is the discount amount and the sale price?

40% = 0.40

$120 × 0.40 = $48

$120 − $48 = $72

Therefore, the discount is $48 and the sale price is $72.

Sales Tax

Sales Tax is an additional amount added to the price of an item. The tax is calculated as a percentage of the original price.

Sales Tax Formula

Tax Amount = Original Price × Tax Rate

Final Price = Original Price + Tax Amount

Sales Tax Example 1

A shirt costs $50. The sales tax is 8%. What is the tax amount and the final price?

8% = 0.08

$50 × 0.08 = $4

$50 + $4 = $54

Therefore, the tax is $4 and the final price is $54.

Sales Tax Example 2

A pair of shoes costs $75. The sales tax is 10%. What is the tax amount and the final price?

10% = 0.10

$75 × 0.10 = $7.50

$75 + $7.50 = $82.50

Therefore, the tax is $7.50 and the final price is $82.50.

Sales Tax Example 3

A laptop costs $800. The sales tax is 7.5%. What is the tax amount and the final price?

7.5% = 0.075

$800 × 0.075 = $60

$800 + $60 = $860

Therefore, the tax is $60 and the final price is $860.

Percent Word Problems

Percent word problems use percentages in real-world situations. Read each problem carefully and identify what information you are given and what you need to find.

Example 1: Finding a Percent of a Number

A school has 400 students. If 35% of the students participate in sports, how many students participate in sports?

35% = 0.35

400 × 0.35 = 140

Therefore, 140 students participate in sports.

Example 2: Discount

A pair of shoes costs $80. The shoes are on sale for 25% off. What is the sale price?

25% = 0.25

$80 × 0.25 = $20

$80 − $20 = $60

Therefore, the sale price is $60.

Example 3: Sales Tax

A backpack costs $50. The sales tax is 8%. What is the final price after tax?

8% = 0.08

$50 × 0.08 = $4

$50 + $4 = $54

Therefore, the final price is $54.

Example 4: Percent Increase

The price of a bicycle increases from $200 to $250. What is the percent increase?

$250 − $200 = $50

(50 ÷ 200) × 100 = 25%

Therefore, the price increased by 25%.

Example 5: Finding the Whole

20% of a number is 30. What is the number?

20% = 0.20

30 ÷ 0.20 = 150

Therefore, the number is 150.
