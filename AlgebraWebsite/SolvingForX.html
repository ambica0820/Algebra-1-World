Solving For X

Some things to take note of:

Key Idea: We need to get X by itself!

Opposite of Multiplication ( × ) is Division ( ÷ )

Opposite of Addition ( + ) is Subtraction ( - )

REMEMBER: What you do to one side, you MUST do to the other side!

Examples:

Example Problem Steps Answer
1 X + 5 = 10 Subtract 5 from both sides.
X - 5 - 5 = X. 10 - 5 = 5.
So we are left with X = 5. 		X= 5
2 3X + 5 = 17 Subtract 5 from both sides.
17 - 5 = 12.
Then divide by 3 on both sides.
3X ÷ 3 = X and
12 ÷ 3 = 4. 		X = 4
3 4X + 3 = 17 Subtract 3 from both sides.
17 - 3 = 14.
Then divide by 4 on both sides.
14 ÷ 4 = 3.5. 		X = 7/2 or 3.5
4 -2X - 8 = 10 Add 8 to both sides.
10 + 8 = 18.
Then divide by -2 on both sides.
18 ÷ -2 = -9. 		X = -9
5 9X + 6 = 21 Subtract 6 from both sides.
21 - 6 = 15.
Then divide by 9 on both sides.
15 ÷ 9 = 5/3. 		X = 15/9 or 5/3
6 8X = 16 Divide by 8 on both sides.
16 ÷ 8 = 2. 		X = 2
